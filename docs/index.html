The live system

The current release. The brain runs on the Reactive Lasna testnet; the vault it protects sits on Base Sepolia. Prices come from the real Chainlink ETH/USD feed. The vault is a per-user pool: anyone can deposit WETH for shares and redeem their own slice at any time — try it in the demo app. The previous flagship set (the trio #5 contracts) stays live and untouched as the previous generation.

The buy-back, measured

What re-risk costs, tested five ways against never buying back

Buy-backs per de-risk episodeat most 2

Every buy-back rule tested lost money on average against never re-entering — it paid in the real recoveries and cost in the real crashes. The shipped two-step rule was the cheapest of the five. It is there so the stop-loss works both ways, not because the backtest says it makes money. The two steps spend half the safe balance at the reclaim, then the rest only on a new high.
