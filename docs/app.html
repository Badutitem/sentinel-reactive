🛡 Sentinel — Demo app

This is the demo you can step into: connect a wallet on the Base Sepolia testnet, wrap a little test ETH and deposit it into the guarded pool — the deposit mints shares in your name, and you can redeem them back out whenever you like. This page reads your own balances, your shares and your claim and the live pool straight from the contracts — the same vault the Sentinel brain watches on the real Chainlink ETH/USD feed.

Where this demo stands, honestly. The app is being opened up one step at a time, and each step ships only after it is proven. Step 1 — wallet connect — is live. Step 2 — deposit — is live. Step 3 — your shares — is live too: the vault keeps per-user share accounting, so a deposit mints shares in your name and step 3 pays them back out, in part or in full, whenever you ask. The one step that stays closed is claiming test mock USDC: that token's mint is owner-only, there is no public faucet, so it is blocked by the contract itself. Step 2 — add test ETH to the guarded position

One button runs the whole flow: wrap ETH into WETH (only the part you are missing), approve the vault for exactly that amount, then deposit. Each step is a real testnet transaction, signed in your wallet, and the position below updates after every confirmed step.

Read this before you deposit — plain facts:
  • Your own shares. A deposit mints shares in your name. Step 3 below shows your shares and what they would pay out right now, read live from the vault.
  • You can take yours back out. Redeem part or all of your shares at any time — no lock, no asking anyone. You are paid your slice of both assets the pool holds at that moment: WETH and mock USDC.
  • One guard, shared decisions. The brain protects the whole pool at once. When it de-risks, the pool's mix shifts toward mock USDC; when it buys back, the mix shifts back toward WETH. Your slice follows the same mix as everyone's, in proportion to your shares — so what a redemption pays out depends on what the guard has done, not only on what you put in.
  • Mock safe asset. The pool de-risks into MockUSDC, a test token — not real USDC. Its mint is owner-only and there is no public faucet.
  • Testnet only, worth nothing. The ETH and WETH here are Base Sepolia test money. They are worth nothing. This page only works on the testnet — never send real funds.
